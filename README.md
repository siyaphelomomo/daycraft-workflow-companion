# Project: SmartWork AI – AI-Powered Productivity Application

## 1. Role
Act as an expert AI productivity assistant who helps users manage emails, summarize meeting notes, organize tasks, and improve time management.

## 2. Objective
Develop a SmartWork AI application with three main features:

**A. Smart Email Generator**
- Generate professional emails for business and workplace communication.
- Allow users to choose different tones, such as formal, friendly, or persuasive.
- Include an email subject, greeting, body, and closing.

**B. Meeting Notes Summarizer**
- Summarize long meeting notes into short, clear summaries.
- Extract important decisions and key points.
- Identify action items, responsible people, and deadlines when provided.
- Present the information in an organized format.

**C. AI Task Planner**
- Generate daily and weekly schedules.
- Prioritize tasks according to urgency and importance.
- Allocate suitable time for each task.
- Include deadlines, reminders, breaks, and unfinished tasks.

## 3. Context
SmartWork AI is designed for students, business owners, employees, and professionals who want to save time and work more efficiently. The application uses artificial intelligence to simplify workplace communication, organize meeting information, and manage daily responsibilities.

The application should be easy to use, user-friendly, and suitable for beginners. Users should be able to enter their information and receive practical, accurate, and organized results.

## 4. Output
The application should provide:

1. **Professional Emails:** Ready-to-edit emails with appropriate subjects, greetings, and closing statements.
2. **Meeting Summaries:** Concise summaries containing key points, decisions, action items, and deadlines.
3. **Daily and Weekly Plans:** Organized schedules with prioritized tasks, estimated durations, and deadlines.

The final application should have a simple dashboard with three main sections: Smart Email Generator, Meeting Notes Summarizer, and AI Task Planner.

The design should be modern, responsive, easy to navigate, and suitable for desktop and mobile devices.

**Expected Result:** A practical AI-powered productivity application that helps users save time, improve communication, organize information, and complete important tasks efficiently.
